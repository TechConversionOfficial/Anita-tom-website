## About the educator

- **Who she is:** Anita Tom, an educator and mentor with a teaching career spanning more than three decades, and founder of Nimble Precise.
- **What she teaches:** her documented teaching background includes mathematics, science, abacus and mental maths, alongside work supporting educators.
- **Who the personal brand speaks to:** parents and students interested in her teaching philosophy, educators interested in professional development, and organisations looking to learn more about her experience.
- **Why this is a separate project:** The Anita Tom website presents the educator's own identity and journey. [Nimble Precise](https://nimbleprecise.com/) represents the education organisation and its student and educator programmes; the two websites should not be presented as one project.

Her professional background includes teaching in India and abacus instruction in Dubai. Her public professional profile also identifies Nimble Precise as her organisation. See [Anita Tom on LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/anitatom).

## The website challenge

A personal-brand website for an experienced educator has a different job from a course catalogue. Visitors need to understand the person behind the work: her background, teaching values, audience and reasons to trust her experience. The project also needs to explain her relationship to Nimble Precise without turning her personal website into a duplicate of the organisation's platform.

Tech Conversion's published portfolio positions Anita Tom as a personal-brand education website. The before-and-after images below show the visual change in the published presentation. They do not, by themselves, establish the findings of a pre-project audit or demonstrate numerical improvements in business performance.

## How the project is positioned

1. **Lead with the educator.** Keep Anita Tom's identity and teaching mission clearer than a generic list of education products.
2. **Make experience meaningful.** Present an established teaching background in a way students, parents and fellow educators can understand.
3. **Speak to two related audiences.** Distinguish support for students from the development of teachers and education partners.
4. **Connect the individual and the institution.** Explain how Anita Tom's personal educational journey relates to her work through Nimble Precise, while keeping the websites distinct.
5. **Give the visitor a next step.** A personal-brand experience should make it straightforward to learn more about her work and reach the relevant education organisation.
6. **Prioritise accessible, responsive presentation.** Mobile layout, legible text, accessible navigation and descriptive image text are appropriate review priorities.

**Documentation boundary:** These are communication and design priorities drawn from the portfolio positioning, not a claim that every implementation detail or test was independently verified on the live website.

## Standards and quality references

The following are relevant review benchmarks for a personal-brand website, not project-specific certifications or completed audit results:

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): page loading, responsiveness and layout stability.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): text contrast, focus visibility, keyboard accessibility and meaningful image alternatives.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): baseline technical and content guidance for search visibility.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): relevant person, organisation and page-type structured information where implemented correctly.
- [India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): relevant data-handling considerations for personal information collected through forms.

No project-specific Lighthouse report, WCAG conformance report, consent audit or validated structured-data report has been supplied with this README.
